# Retail Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built on retail sales data covering the period from January 2016 to December 2018. The goal was to break down sales performance across customer segments, cities, and products, and to give business users an easy way to filter and drill through the numbers without touching the raw data.

The dataset includes customer, order, product, staff, and store level information, which I combined into a single data model to build out the visuals.

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop (data modeling, DAX, visuals)
- Power Query (data cleaning and transformation)
- DAX (calculated measures for KPIs and aggregations)

## Key Features
- KPI summary cards for total quantity sold, average order value, total revenue, total orders, and total customers
- Customer segmentation (Low, Regular, High) shown by both total sales value and customer count
- City-wise sales comparison to identify top-performing locations
- Product-wise sales breakdown to spot best sellers
- Slicers for order status, product name, store name, and customer name

## Key Insights
- The Low segment accounts for the majority of both revenue and customer count, making it the most important group to focus retention efforts on
- Ballston Spa, Mount Vernon, and Howard Beach came out as the top three cities by total sales
- A small set of products drive a large share of revenue, which points to where inventory and marketing focus should go
